Could You Simplify?
You could fall back to relay-only for important state and accept eventual consistency:

Ledger entries always go to relays (authoritative history)
Real-time balance updates via WebRTC if peer is online
If WebRTC fails or doesn't come back after 30s, fall back to relay sync
Then WebRTC becomes optional (a performance optimization), not critical. Failures wouldn't break the app, just make it slower.

That would let you:

*Rip out most of the wake-from-suspend logic*
Accept that reconnects take 10-20s
Not care about "stale green state" because relay sync is the real source of truth
The UX would be: "friend appears online (relay + WebRTC) or offline (relay only, 1-2s latency)." Still feels good, no nightmare.
