HOW IT WORKS

Like a tally stick —
split between two.

1
Connect with people you trust
Add friends by scanning their QR code or by sharing your identity key.
2
Record a favour
Did someone cover your coffee? Give you their unused table? Log a tally. It's kept on your phone, not on any server. In time tallies cancel each other out and you can always see your balance with your friends.
3
Let it flow through the community
Your favours can travel through chains of trust to someone you don't even know. When loops form, they cancel automatically. The whole network stays in balance.

Every favour logged has a matching entry somewhere in the network — always balanced, always honest.

THE CONCEPT

Mutual favour — as old as community itself.

Before coins and central banks, communities made trade based on trust and shared records. In the digital age we can extend this local trust to global communities.

Choose your currency
The value of favours can be recorded in any currency you and your friends choose: euros, work hours, meals. Only imagination is the limit.
Secure and private
Tallies are secured with modern cryptography, so no one besides the people making an exchange can see them.
Open source and decentralised
Tally is not just an app, it's a protocol that anyone can build on. The local nature of tallies makes it truly decentralised, while also allowing it to scale without any bottlenecks.
Circular cancellation
If A owes B and B owes C and C owes A, Tally cancels the loop automatically. Debts dissolve; the community moves on.
CHAINED TRUST

Reach anyone through
a chain of friends.

Alice and Bob trust each other. Bob and Charlie trust each other. Alice needs to pay Charlie — but they've never met.

Tally routes the favour through Bob: Alice records a tally with Bob, Bob records one of equal value with Charlie. Charlie is paid. Bob's balance stays neutral. Alice and Charlie can transact without having to trust each other.

Chains can be as long as needed — the more trust connections in your community, the further a favour can travel.

THE ROOTS

Money was never meant to be this complicated.

Whether Hawala, Quipu or Tally Sticks, using records of good deeds as money is long and ancient. When you root money in the community it stays honest, and can't support wars or other destructive aims.

Tally.earth returns this idea to the digital age — and takes it further. By chaining tallies, local trust can extend to global trade. No central monetary system. No manipulation of the money supply. No corruption.

"Money, in its essence, is just bookkeeping that tracks good deeds that should be paid back."

— Tally manifesto
