* Trust limit is the largest amount you feel comfortable loaning or being in debt to this person.

Trust limit agreements are used when sending IOU's between people who don't know each other. Trust agreements won't change your total IOU balance, but they allow exchanging IOU's you have with one of your friend to IOU's of another one of your friends. With the help of trust agreements you can trade with friends friends friend, without knowing or having to trust that person yourself.
