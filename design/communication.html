Communication

How peers find each other and move data. Tally prefers a direct encrypted peer-to-peer channel and falls back to its own relay when that can't form — but it never trusts the transport for authenticity; that's Security's job.

The connection path

  • Direct (WebRTC). Peers form a mesh of WebRTC data channels — a friend mesh (per friend device) and a self-mesh (your own devices). STUN helps them find a route across networks; there is no TURN server.
  • Relay fallback. The relay server does WebRTC signaling and, when a direct channel can't form or a peer is offline, store-and-forwards the encrypted envelopes (waking the recipient with a Web Push hint).

What flows

Outbound messages queue in the outbox and travel as encrypted envelopes. When two peers connect they sync — reconciling their ledgers so a reconnecting or recovering device catches up. Transport-only messages (ping, sync, receipts) are distinct from durable records and carry no authorship.
