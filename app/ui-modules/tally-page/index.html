In Tally, you don't need to reciprocate each individual favour. What matters is the combined tally of all records.

If your total tally is negative and worryingly large, it is time to start giving more to your community. On the other hand, if your tally is a lot on the positive side, you can relax and enjoy the bounty of your past good deeds.
